        VAULT-TEC WEAPONS WORKBENCH

        Weapon Mod Lab

        Pick the exact weapon platform you are holding. The guide explains what each mod slot changes, which perks craft advanced parts, what build the weapon suits, and how to scavenge the attachment instead of crafting it.

        Weapon catalog

        1 · CRAFT IT

        At a Weapons Workbench, advanced ballistic mods usually use Gun Nut, energy/high-tech mods use Science!, and melee mods use Blacksmith. Some high-end mods require two perk families.

        2 · STEAL THE MOD

        Find or buy the same weapon with the attachment already installed. Replace that attachment with a cheap/basic one; the removed part becomes a loose mod you can reuse.

        3 · DON'T SCRAP TOO FAST

        A boring enemy gun may be carrying an expensive suppressor, scope, receiver or stock. Check heavily-modified loot before scrapping or selling it.

        Important weapon-perk rule: the attachment can change which damage perk applies. A semi-auto rifle/shotgun uses Rifleman; an automatic receiver uses Commando; a non-automatic pistol uses Gunslinger. On Pipe/Laser/Plasma families, changing grip ↔ stock can also change pistol ↔ rifle classification.
        Receiver / CapacitorUsually the biggest damage change; can also switch a gun to automatic.
        BarrelLong = range/accuracy. Short/light = handling and often friendlier V.A.T.S. cost.
        Grip / StockControls recoil/handling and, on convertible weapons, whether pistol or rifle perks apply.
        Sight / ScopeReflex is easy general/V.A.T.S. use. Long/recon scopes are for deliberate sniping.
        MagazineCapacity vs reload speed. Quick-eject options are often a very comfortable middle ground.
        MuzzleSuppressor = stealth. Compensator/brake-style parts = recoil control when you do not care about noise.
