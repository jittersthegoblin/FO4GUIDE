HOW TO READ IT

SPECIAL is an unlock threshold

Example: Ninja sits at AGI 7. You need base Agility 7 to buy Ninja, but you do not need to purchase all of the Agility perks above it first.
Starting vs. later: your starting character only has 28 total SPECIAL points. You can spend later level-up perk points directly on SPECIAL training, so a long-term target of 8 or 9 does not mean you must start there.
Weapon rule: damage perks follow weapon class. A laser rifle can use Rifleman or Commando depending on its receiver; “energy weapon” itself is not one universal damage perk.
